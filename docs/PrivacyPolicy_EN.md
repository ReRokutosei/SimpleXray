# Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0  
Effective Date: August 10, 2026

## 1. Core Privacy Principles
SimpleXray adheres strictly to the principles of **Zero Remote Data Collection** and **Local Execution**. As a personal research codebase, the Software is designed without any mechanisms to collect, track, or leak user privacy or device data to remote servers.

## 2. Zero Data Collection & Logging
The Software incorporates no developer telemetry, analytics, or background tracking APIs:
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect names, contact details, unique device IDs, locations, or identity attributes.
- **Config & Local Data**: Research configuration files remain stored strictly within your Android device's private app sandbox.
- **Activity Monitoring**: The developer does not monitor, log, or inspect user interactions or runtime operations.

## 3. System Permissions & Sandbox Isolation
Permissions requested by the Software are used strictly for local Android platform functionality. All preferences and configuration data are confined within local sandbox storage, protected by Android operating system process isolation.

## 4. Open Source Auditing & Contact
As a fully open-source research project, the codebase is transparent and open for security and privacy audits at any time. For technical discussions or privacy inquiries, feel free to open an issue on the official GitHub repository.
